### How to read this

- **Flow (est)** = dollar volume x agreement x conviction, where agreement is the average of (where the close sits in the day's range) and (the sign of the move), and conviction is the size of the move relative to that instrument's own ATR. All three have to line up before a day counts as real inflow. It is an **estimate from price and volume**, not ETF creation/redemption data, not 13F positioning, and not net cash entering a security.
- **RVOL** is today's dollar volume divided by the 20-session median. Above ~1.5x means real participation.
- **Share of move** is each name's slice of traded dollars times its move, normalised across the equity universe. It shows who dominated activity today; it is not official index weight or index points.
- **Risk appetite** ranks a risk-on basket (SPY, IWM, HYG, KRE, XLY, BTC) against a risk-off basket (TLT, GLD, DXY, VIX, XLP, XLU) over 5 sessions, each against its own 1-year history. 50 = neutral.
